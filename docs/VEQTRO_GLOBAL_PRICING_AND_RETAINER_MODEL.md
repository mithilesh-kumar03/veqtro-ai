## 4. Payment Terms & Invoicing Protocols

To maintain rigorous engineering delivery standards, all client engagements strictly follow these commercial terms:

1. **Setup Fee Milestone Schedule**:
   - **50% Upfront**: Payable upon project kickoff and agreement execution, prior to commencement of any architectural or engineering work.
   - **50% Handover Milestone**: Payable upon completion of system implementation and verification, immediately prior to final production handover, credential transfer, or live system activation.
2. **Monthly Retainer Schedule**:
   - Monthly retainers are billed **in advance** for each 30-day service cycle.
   - Retainer billing begins immediately following successful implementation, testing, and system handover.
3. **Payment Window**:
   - All invoices are strictly payable within **7 calendar days** of invoice issuance unless otherwise agreed in writing.
4. **Taxation**:
   - All quoted rates are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST). Applicable GST (currently 18%) is added to all invoices.
5. **Third-Party & Out-of-Scope Expenses**:
   - Third-party subscriptions (Meta WhatsApp Cloud API conversation credits, CRM subscription user seats, domain registration, and hosting compute) and advertising expenditure are funded directly by the client.
   - Out-of-scope feature requests, additional integrations, or engineering hours exceeding the package monthly allocation are quoted separately and require prior client written approval before work begins.
6. **Overdue Invoices & Service Suspension**:
   - If an invoice remains unpaid past the 7-day payment window, VEQTRO will issue a formal written reminder.
   - If payment is not cleared within 5 business days following written notice, VEQTRO reserves the right to temporarily pause active webhook relays, support availability, and retainer engineering services until accounts are fully settled.

## 5. Cancellation, Termination & Refund Policy

1. **Retainer Cancellation Notice**:
   - Clients may cancel their ongoing monthly retainer at any time by providing **30 calendar days' written notice** to `growth.veqtro@gmail.com`.
2. **Notice Period Obligations**:
   - The monthly retainer remains payable during the 30-day notice period.
   - VEQTRO continues to provide all ongoing monitoring, SLA uptime, and support services throughout this period unless agreed otherwise in writing.
3. **Setup Fee Non-Refundability**:
   - One-time setup fees are **strictly non-refundable** once implementation work has commenced, as engineering time, architecture planning, and resource allocation are committed immediately upon contract signing, except where required by applicable consumer protection law or agreed otherwise in writing.
4. **Settlement on Termination**:
   - Payments for completed implementation milestones, services delivered, and approved third-party expenses incurred up to the effective termination date remain fully payable.
   - Upon termination and subject to complete settlement of all outstanding invoices, VEQTRO will deliver all agreed completed deliverables, configurations, and documentation to the client.
5. **Post-Termination Transition & Data Export**:
   - The client retains ownership of all customer conversation logs, CRM records, and intellectual assets.
   - Any custom data export, platform migration, or extended transition assistance beyond the standard handover scope will be quoted separately as a one-time project fee.
6. **Termination for Material Breach**:
   - Either party may terminate the agreement with immediate effect if the other party commits a material breach of terms that remains unresolved after **15 calendar days' written notice** specifying the breach.
7. **Refund Handling**:
   - Prepaid monthly retainer fees are not automatically refundable. Any applicable prorated credit or refund will be assessed strictly according to the executed agreement, deliverables completed, and applicable statutory law.
