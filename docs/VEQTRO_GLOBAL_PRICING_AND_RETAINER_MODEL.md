## 1. Commercial Structure & Service Ecosystem

VEQTRO provides three modular, independently purchasable, but complementary core development service pillars:

1. **Service A — Website Development:** Building high-performance public digital presence that clearly articulates value and captures inquiries.
2. **Service B — Custom CRM Development:** Building tailored customer relationship and pipeline systems that organize leads and team workflows.
3. **Service C — Backend Revenue Engine:** Building connective backend infrastructure, webhooks, API bridges, and automation that power business operations.

Clients may purchase any single service, any combination of two services, or the complete full-stack solution (all three services).

## 4. Combined Service Discount (The Ecosystem Incentive)

Clients who contract all three core development services together receive a dedicated bundle incentive:

$$\mathbf{10\%\ Off\ the\ Combined\ One\text{-}Time\ Development\ Fees}$$

### Rules of Application:
- **Eligibility:** Valid **only** when Service A (Website), Service B (CRM), and Service C (Backend Revenue Engine) are contracted concurrently in a single master statement of work.
- **Application:** Deducts 10% from the total sum of the three one-time implementation development fees.
- **Exclusions:** The discount does **not** apply to monthly retainers, ongoing support plans, statutory taxes (GST), or third-party expenses.
- **Non-Stackable:** Cannot be combined with other promotional credits, referral reductions, or special commercial agreements.

## 5. Payment Terms & Invoicing Protocols

1. **Milestone Advance Schedule:**
   - **50% Project Advance:** Payable upon contract execution, before development commences.
   - **50% Completion Milestone:** Payable upon completion of all scoped deliverables and passing the QA Gate, before final source code handover, credential transfer, or live production activation.
2. **Monthly Retainer Invoicing:**
   - Retainers are billed **in advance** for each 30-day service cycle, starting immediately after project handover.
3. **Payment Window:**
   - Invoices are strictly payable within **7 calendar days** of issuance unless otherwise agreed in writing.
4. **Taxes:**
   - All quoted fees are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST). Applicable GST (18%) is added to all invoices.
5. **Third-Party & Pass-Through Expenses:**
   - Client provides and pays directly for hosting compute, domain registration, commercial software user licenses, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API conversation credits, and ad spend.
6. **Overdue Invoices:**
   - Late payments past 7 calendar days receive formal written notice. If unresolved within 5 business days following notice, VEQTRO reserves the right to pause active services.

## 6. Cancellation & Termination Policy

1. **Retainer Cancellation:**
   - Clients may cancel monthly retainers by providing **30 calendar days' written notice** to `growth.veqtro@gmail.com`.
   - The retainer remains payable during the 30-day notice period, and services continue active throughout that window.
2. **Development Fee Non-Refundability:**
   - One-time development fees are **non-refundable** once implementation work has commenced, as engineering time and architectural resources are committed immediately upon project kickoff, except where required by applicable consumer law or agreed otherwise in writing.
3. **Settlement on Termination:**
   - Fees for completed milestones, delivered work, and approved expenses remain payable. Completed deliverables are transferred upon full settlement.
4. **Transition Assistance:**
   - Data export or migration assistance beyond standard handover scope will be quoted separately.
5. **Termination for Material Breach:**
   - Either party may terminate for an unresolved material breach after **15 calendar days' written notice**.
6. **Refund Administration:**
   - Prepaid monthly retainers are not automatically refundable; handled strictly per contract and statutory law.
