Governance & Data Security

Enterprise Legal Architecture

1. Zero-Retention & Data Sovereignty

VEQTRO operates as an infrastructure transit layer. Prospect information captured through Meta Lead Ads, WhatsApp Cloud API, and web flagships is securely dispatched via end-to-end encrypted webhooks directly into your proprietary CRM (LeadSquared, Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho). VEQTRO does not sell, broker, monetize, or retain client lead records on persistent storage.

2. WhatsApp Business & Meta Tech Tier Compliance

All automated Speed-to-Lead intake workflows adhere strictly to the Meta WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy. Automated replies, qualification flows, and appointment reminders are initiated only upon explicit user opt-in through lead form submission or direct message initiation. Opt-out mechanisms (e.g., replying 'STOP') are processed with instant cessation SLAs.

3. Indian DPDP Act (2023) & Global GDPR Protocols

For Indian enterprise deployments, all webhook data handling complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023. European and UK enterprise deployments operate under strict GDPR Data Processing Addendums (DPA) with regional data residency guarantees.

4. Encryption Standards

All payload transmissions utilize TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. Bi-directional CRM webhook secret signatures and authentication tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 key management.

1. Scope of Managed Infrastructure

VEQTRO engineers, deploys, and monitors bespoke growth infrastructure for high-ticket clients. Deliverables include sub-10 second speed-to-lead qualification engines, bi-directional CRM webhook synchronization, offline field sales PWAs, and custom revenue estimators.

2. 99.98% Intake Engine SLA

VEQTRO guarantees a 99.98% uptime SLA on automated webhook intake relays. Dedicated senior infrastructure engineers monitor latency round-the-clock with automated fallback failover pipelines.

3. 100% Client Intellectual Property & Data Ownership

The client retains exclusive and perpetual ownership of all CRM records, customer conversation histories, brand assets, and custom qualification state machines deployed into their infrastructure.

4. Deployment Cap & White-Glove Support

Deployments are strictly capped at 4 enterprise rollouts per month to ensure dedicated infrastructure engineering capacity and white-glove onboarding for every client.
